# Camp Fire — GDD

## Concept

Manage a campsite that grows around a magical fire (Spark of Ara). Grow plants, manage resources, and expand the camp using Mana.

## Resources

- **Mana:** Passively generated by the flame. Its size and color determine production rate. Used for crafting.
- **Water:** Stored in vases that are filled by sending Mallum to collect water. Required for watering. You start with 2 vases.

## Systems

- **Flame:** The heart of the camp. Controls how many plots you can have.
- **Vases:** Craftable and upgradeable with Mana. Customizable in color and decoration.
- **Plots:** Plant seeds and they grow after some time. If weather conditions were suitable, you get a higher reward. You harvest and the plot clears — you can plant something new.
- **Gardens:** Plant seeds and they grow after some time. They result in a tree/shrub that periodically yields fruit.
- **Apotheke:** A wooden cabinet with small packets. Store seeds, analyze them, and mix them to create new species.
- **Real Weather:** Linked to your region in real time. Affects growth speed and/or crop quality/quantity.
  - Variables: temperature, humidity, wind, rain, lunar phase.

## Interface (Top-Down)

- **Top bar:** Current weather + expandable forecast.
- **Bottom bar:** Apotheke | Letters | Craft.

## Intro

Animated panels: a Mallum (assistant to the archdruid Argus) finds the Spark of Ara in the swamp, shields it with branches, and the game begins.

## Core Loop

Flame generates Mana → craft plots and vases → plant seeds → water them → real weather determines growth → the camp expands.

## Meta-Progression

Fertilizers, new ceramic colors made with flowers, potions that briefly alter the weather. (Obtained in the Apotheke by mixing elements: energy, water, flowers.)

## Social

Send/receive seeds and flowers between friends via Letters.
