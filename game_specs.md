# Camp Fire — GDD

## Concept

Manage a campsite that grows around a magical fire (the Spark of Ara). Grow plants, dispatch Mallum helpers on quests, manage resources, and expand your hex-grid campsite. Uses real-world weather data to influence plant growth and quality. Online-only with a server-authoritative economy.

## Resources

- **Mana** (float): Passively generated by the flame. Rate and cap scale with flame level. Spent on crafting plots, vases, gardens, mallum houses, and upgrades.
- **Water** (int): Stored in vases. Filled by sending a Mallum to collect water. Spent on watering plants.
- **Gems** (int): Premium currency.

New players start with 50 mana, 5 gems, 1 vase (full), 1 plot, 5 Sprouts + 3 Cress seeds, and initial Mallums.

## Core Loop

Flame generates Mana -> craft plots and vases -> plant seeds -> water them -> real weather determines growth quality -> harvest -> expand the camp -> upgrade the flame -> unlock more.

## Systems

### Flame

The heart of the camp. Controls progression through flame level:
- **Mana generation**: Passive mana per second, rate and cap scale with level.
- **Entity cap**: Plots, vases, gardens, and mallum houses share a single entity cap that increases with level.
- **Grid size**: Hex grid radius expands with level.
- **Upgrades**: Costs inventory items (from `FlameUpgradeRecipe`). Server-first operation.

### Plots

Single-harvest planting cycle:
1. **Craft** a plot on the hex grid (costs mana, gated by entity cap).
2. **Plant** a seed (consumes seed from inventory).
3. **Water** (spends water from a vase, starts the growth timer, begins recording weather snapshots).
4. **Harvest** when mature — quality score (0-1) determined by GrowthRecipe evaluation of recorded weather vs. ideal conditions.

The plot clears after harvest and can be replanted.

### Vases

Water storage containers. Craftable and upgradeable with mana. Filling a vase requires sending a Mallum to fetch water (always goes through `MallumManager.SendToFetchWater`, never directly). Fill time is configurable. Can be sped up with Energy Drink consumables.

### Gardens

Permanent plants unlocked at flame level 4. Placed on the hex grid (share entity cap).
1. **Craft** an empty garden (costs mana + harvest items).
2. **Plant** (consumes seed + water).
3. Growth over configured hours -> becomes mature.
4. **Yields** fruit periodically (every `yieldIntervalHours`). Collect yields to get items.
5. **Fertilize** with a fertilizer item for +50% bonus on next yield.

### Mallums

Helper creatures that perform tasks. Count is gated by mallum houses (built on the hex grid).

**States**: Idle, FetchingWater, OnQuest, QuestComplete.

**Actions**:
- **Fetch water**: Claim an idle Mallum, send to fill a vase.
- **Send on quest**: Claim an idle Mallum, start a timed quest. Duration in minutes from quest config.
- **Collect rewards**: After quest completes, roll weighted random rewards from the quest's reward pool. Player explicitly collects to add seeds/items to inventory.
- **Speed up**: Both quests and water fetches can be sped up with Energy Drink consumables.
- **Build house**: Costs mana + harvest items. Each house increases max Mallum count.

### GrowthRecipe (Weather Quality)

Each seed has a recipe defining ideal growing conditions across multiple axes: heat, wind, humidity, sunlight, rain, moon phase, and waterings. During growth, weather snapshots are periodically recorded. At harvest, the recipe evaluates snapshots against ideal ranges to produce a 0-1 quality score (weighted average). Full score within ideal range, linear falloff within tolerance, zero beyond.

A recipe with no enabled axes returns 1.0 (perfect score).

### Apotheke

Seed storage and mixing station. Store seeds, view inventory, and mix seeds using recipes to create new species. Recipes loaded from `Resources/Recipes/`.

### Real Weather

Linked to the player's real-world location via GPS + OpenWeatherMap API. Affects plant growth quality through GrowthRecipe.

Variables: temperature, humidity, wind speed, rain, sunlight (cloud cover), lunar phase, calendar events (equinoxes, eclipses).

Weather is fetched every 15 minutes on device. In the Unity Editor, debug weather override mode is used instead of GPS/API calls.

### Skins

Cosmetic skins for buildings (plots, vases, mallum houses). Purchased per-building using inventory items. Once unlocked on a building, can be re-applied for free. Changes hex fill and border colors. Loaded from `SkinData` ScriptableObjects.

## Interface

### Floating HUD

Floating pill-shaped elements positioned on screen:
- **Weather icon**: Tap to open forecast bloom card (today's stats + multi-day forecast).
- **Profile icon**: Tap to open profile bloom card (display name, friend code, flame level, audio sliders, language, settings, delete save).
- **Resource counters**: Mana, water, and available/total Mallums with icons.

A shared backdrop dismisses any open bloom card when tapped.

### Bottom Navigation

Buttons that open slide-up overlay panels:
- **Apotheke**: Seed inventory and mixing.
- **Letters**: Social — send/receive seeds and flowers between friends.
- **Build/Craft**: Build new plots, vases, gardens, mallum houses. Upgrade flame.
- **Quests**: Send Mallums on quests, collect rewards.

### Campsite View

Top-down hex grid showing the campsite. Tap hexes to interact with placed entities (water plots, collect vase, harvest crops, etc.). Pans when grid exceeds screen size. Lighting overlay changes with time of day. Weather VFX overlay (rain, snow particles).

### Loading Gate

Full-screen gate shown until Social, Economy, Game, and Weather services are ready. Progress bar with stall warning after 30s and server selector on failure.

## Visitors

Server-driven nightly visitors. One visitor per day, appearing after 10 PM local time.

**Three types**:
- **Merchant**: Offers trades (costs inventory items, rewards other items).
- **Gifter**: Brings a free gift (water, seeds, or items).
- **Quester**: Requests specific items. Returns after a configurable number of days with a reward if the player accepts and has the items.

Visitors are placed on a random free hex tile. On arrival, a dialogue plays, then the visitor interaction modal opens. Visitors depart at end of visitor hours or next day.

## Birds

Hourly random seed drops. Birds appear on free hex tiles and can be tapped to collect seeds.

- Spawn chance decays with more uncollected birds on the grid (halving factor).
- Eligible seeds: all seeds with tier <= flame level.
- Drop quantity scales with flame level relative to seed tier.
- Server-driven when online, local RNG fallback when offline.

## Tutorial

13-step linear tutorial (steps 0-12) guiding new players through core mechanics:

0. **Welcome** — Dialogue from Spark of Ara.
1. **Plant First** — Plant a seed in the starting plot.
2. **Water First** — Water the plant (growth paused briefly to give time).
3. **Harvest First** — Wait for maturity, harvest the crop.
4. **Build House** — Build a Mallum House.
5. **Plant Again** — Plant another seed.
6. **Fetch Water** — Send Mallum to fetch water (growth capped at 60%).
7. **Send on Quest** — Send Mallum on a quest.
8. **Speed Up Quest** — Use Energy Drink to speed up.
9. **Plant Cress** — Plant Cress and harvest it.
10. **Build Second Plot** — Build another plot.
11. **Upgrade Flame** — Open flame upgrade menu.
12. **Complete** — Tutorial done.

Growth is paused during early steps. Quest rewards guarantee at least 1 Cress seed during tutorial.

## Audio

Music source (looped) + 4-slot SFX pool (round-robin) with pitch randomization. Uses Unity AudioMixer with "Music" and "SFX" groups. Volume persisted in save data. Clips loaded from `SoundLibrary.asset`.

## Social

- Send/receive seeds and flowers between friends via Letters.
- Friend codes for adding friends.
- Auto-registration on first launch (no explicit sign-in).
- Village snapshots pushed to server on every save.
- Deep link support for invite codes.

## Server Architecture

Online-only with server-authoritative economy. Elixir/Phoenix backend with Postgres.

- **ConfigService**: All game tuning (seeds, quests, gardens, flame, vases, mallum houses, building costs) fetched from server. No local config ScriptableObjects.
- **EconomyService**: Queues economy actions and syncs with server endpoints.
- **GameService**: Orchestrates initialization, exposes online/initialized flags.
- **Server-served sprites**: All game sprites served from the backend, cached locally. Sprite manifest tracks content hashes for incremental updates.
- **Localization**: Locale strings fetched from server.

## Meta-Progression

Fertilizers, new ceramic colors made with flowers, potions that briefly alter the weather. Obtained in the Apotheke by mixing elements.
